# NBH-CARD-014 — Advanced Cardiac Imaging

Northbeck Health Plan Medical Policy
Effective: 2026-01-01 | Version: 4.2 | Service line: Cardiology
Review cycle: annual | Next scheduled review: 2027-01-01

## NBH-CARD-014-1 — Scope

This policy addresses coverage of cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (CMR) and
coronary computed tomography angiography (CCTA) performed for the evaluation of
known or suspected coronary artery disease, cardiomyopathy, or myocardial
infiltrative disease. Nuclear stress imaging is addressed separately under
NBH-CARD-011.

## NBH-CARD-014-2 — Coverage Position

Northbeck Health Plan considers cardiac magnetic resonance imaging medically
necessary when the criteria in section 3 are documented. Coronary computed
tomography angiography is considered medically necessary when the criteria in
section 4 are documented.

## NBH-CARD-014-3 — Coverage Criteria: Cardiac Magnetic Resonance Imaging

All of the following must be documented in the member's medical record:

### NBH-CARD-014-3.1

The member has symptoms consistent with cardiac disease, including but not
limited to chest pain, dyspnoea on exertion, syncope, or palpitations, that have
persisted for at least six weeks, or that are documented as progressive.

### NBH-CARD-014-3.2

An initial diagnostic evaluation has been completed and documented. This
evaluation must include a twelve-lead electrocardiogram and a transthoracic
echocardiogram performed within the twelve months preceding the request.

### NBH-CARD-014-3.3

The results of the initial evaluation under section 3.2 are inconclusive,
equivocal, or technically inadequate for the clinical question posed, and the
treating clinician has documented what remains unresolved.

### NBH-CARD-014-3.4

The requested study is expected to change clinical management, and the treating
clinician has documented the specific management decision that depends on the
result.

### NBH-CARD-014-3.5

There is no contraindication to magnetic resonance imaging, or, where a relative
contraindication exists, the medical record documents that it has been addressed.

## NBH-CARD-014-4 — Coverage Criteria: Coronary CT Angiography

All of the following must be documented in the member's medical record:

### NBH-CARD-014-4.1

The member presents with stable chest pain and an intermediate pre-test
probability of coronary artery disease, as documented by a validated risk
assessment tool.

### NBH-CARD-014-4.2

The member has not undergone coronary revascularisation, and no coronary stent
is in place, unless the requested study is for the evaluation of graft patency.

### NBH-CARD-014-4.3

The member's heart rate is expected to be controllable to a rate suitable for
the acquisition, or the imaging facility has documented capability for
high-heart-rate acquisition.

## NBH-CARD-014-5 — Documentation Requirements

### NBH-CARD-014-5.1

A clinical note from the treating clinician dated within six months of the
request describing the presenting symptoms, their duration, and the clinical
question the study is intended to answer.

### NBH-CARD-014-5.2

The report of the transthoracic echocardiogram relied upon under section 3.2,
including its date and its stated limitations where applicable.

### NBH-CARD-014-5.3

The twelve-lead electrocardiogram report relied upon under section 3.2,
including its date.

## NBH-CARD-014-6 — Exclusions

### NBH-CARD-014-6.1

Advanced cardiac imaging performed solely for screening in an asymptomatic
member, absent a documented high-risk inherited cardiomyopathy in a
first-degree relative.

### NBH-CARD-014-6.2

Repeat advanced cardiac imaging of the same anatomical territory within twelve
months of a prior diagnostic-quality study, absent a documented interval change
in clinical status.

### NBH-CARD-014-6.3

Advanced cardiac imaging performed for research purposes or as part of a
clinical trial protocol funded by the trial sponsor.

## NBH-CARD-014-7 — Appeal Rights

An adverse determination under this policy may be appealed in writing within
180 days of the date of the determination notice. A first-level appeal receives
a written response within 30 calendar days. A peer-to-peer discussion with a
Northbeck medical director may be requested by the treating clinician at any
point during the appeal period.
