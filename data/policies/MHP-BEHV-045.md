# MHP-BEHV-045 — Intensive Outpatient Programmes for Behavioral Health

Meridian Health Plan Medical Policy
Effective: 2026-01-01 | Version: 1.8 | Service line: Behavioral Health
Review cycle: annual | Next scheduled review: 2027-01-01

## MHP-BEHV-045-1 — Scope

This policy addresses coverage of intensive outpatient programmes (IOP) for the
treatment of mental health and substance use disorders in members aged eighteen
and over. Partial hospitalisation is addressed under MHP-BEHV-046, and standard
outpatient therapy is not subject to prior authorisation.

## MHP-BEHV-045-2 — Coverage Position

Meridian Health Plan considers an intensive outpatient programme medically
necessary when the criteria in section 3 are documented. Initial authorisation
is for up to four weeks, with continuation subject to section 5.

## MHP-BEHV-045-3 — Coverage Criteria

All of the following must be documented in the member's medical record:

### MHP-BEHV-045-3.1

The member has a documented diagnosis of a mental health or substance use
disorder made by a qualified clinician, recorded with the diagnostic criteria
supporting it.

### MHP-BEHV-045-3.2

A standardised assessment of severity has been completed and documented within
the thirty days preceding the request, using an instrument appropriate to the
diagnosis.

### MHP-BEHV-045-3.3

The member's symptoms cause significant impairment in at least one major life
domain, being occupational, academic, social, or self-care functioning, and the
impairment is described specifically rather than asserted.

### MHP-BEHV-045-3.4

A less intensive level of care has been attempted and documented as
insufficient, or a less intensive level of care is documented as clinically
inappropriate with the reasoning stated.

### MHP-BEHV-045-3.5

The member does not currently require twenty-four-hour supervision, and no
acute risk requiring inpatient admission is present at the time of the request.

### MHP-BEHV-045-3.6

The member is able to participate in and adhere to a structured programme, and
any barriers to participation are documented with the plan to address them.

## MHP-BEHV-045-4 — Documentation Requirements

### MHP-BEHV-045-4.1

A clinical assessment dated within thirty days of the request stating the
diagnosis, the severity instrument used and its score, and the functional
impairment.

### MHP-BEHV-045-4.2

A treatment plan with measurable goals and the anticipated duration of the
programme.

### MHP-BEHV-045-4.3

Documentation of prior treatment at a less intensive level of care, with dates
and outcome, where section 3.4 is met on that basis.

## MHP-BEHV-045-5 — Continuation of Coverage

Continuation beyond the initial authorisation requires documentation of
attendance, a repeat administration of the severity instrument used under
section 3.2, and a statement of progress against the treatment plan goals.

## MHP-BEHV-045-6 — Exclusions

### MHP-BEHV-045-6.1

Programmes whose primary component is educational, vocational, or recreational
rather than clinical.

### MHP-BEHV-045-6.2

Court-ordered treatment where no independent clinical indication is documented.

## MHP-BEHV-045-7 — Appeal Rights

An adverse determination under this policy may be appealed in writing within
180 days of the date of the determination notice. A first-level appeal receives
a written response within 30 calendar days. Where the determination concerns an
urgent clinical situation, an expedited appeal decided within 72 hours may be
requested.
