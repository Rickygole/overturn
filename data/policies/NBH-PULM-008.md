# NBH-PULM-008 — Positive Airway Pressure Devices for Obstructive Sleep Apnea

Northbeck Health Plan Medical Policy
Effective: 2026-01-01 | Version: 2.4 | Service line: Pulmonology
Review cycle: annual | Next scheduled review: 2027-01-01

## NBH-PULM-008-1 — Scope

This policy addresses coverage of continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP)
and automatic positive airway pressure (APAP) devices, together with their
supplies, for the treatment of obstructive sleep apnea in adult members.
Bi-level devices are addressed under NBH-PULM-009.

## NBH-PULM-008-2 — Coverage Position

Northbeck Health Plan considers a positive airway pressure device medically
necessary for an adult member when the criteria in section 3 are documented.
Initial authorisation is for ninety days, with continuation subject to
section 5.

## NBH-PULM-008-3 — Coverage Criteria

All of the following must be documented in the member's medical record:

### NBH-PULM-008-3.1

The member has undergone a sleep study, either an in-laboratory polysomnogram
or a home sleep apnea test, interpreted by a qualified clinician, within the
twenty-four months preceding the request.

### NBH-PULM-008-3.2

The sleep study documents an apnea-hypopnea index of 15 or more events per
hour; or an apnea-hypopnea index of 5 to 14 events per hour accompanied by at
least one of: documented excessive daytime sleepiness, hypertension, ischaemic
heart disease, or a history of stroke.

### NBH-PULM-008-3.3

A face-to-face clinical evaluation addressing sleep-disordered breathing has
been performed and documented prior to the sleep study.

### NBH-PULM-008-3.4

The member has received, or is scheduled to receive, instruction in the use and
maintenance of the device and its interface.

## NBH-PULM-008-4 — Documentation Requirements

### NBH-PULM-008-4.1

The complete sleep study report, including the apnea-hypopnea index, the study
date, and the interpreting clinician.

### NBH-PULM-008-4.2

The clinical note documenting the face-to-face evaluation required under
section 3.3, with its date.

## NBH-PULM-008-5 — Continuation of Coverage

Continuation beyond the initial ninety days requires documentation of adherence,
defined as use of the device for at least four hours per night on seventy
percent of nights during a consecutive thirty-day period within the first
ninety days, together with a clinical note documenting benefit.

## NBH-PULM-008-6 — Exclusions

### NBH-PULM-008-6.1

Positive airway pressure devices for central sleep apnea, which are addressed
under NBH-PULM-012.

### NBH-PULM-008-6.2

Replacement of a device before the end of its five-year reasonable useful
lifetime, absent documented irreparable damage or a documented change in
clinical requirement.

## NBH-PULM-008-7 — Appeal Rights

An adverse determination under this policy may be appealed in writing within
180 days of the date of the determination notice. A first-level appeal receives
a written response within 30 calendar days.
