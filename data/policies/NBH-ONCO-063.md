# NBH-ONCO-063 — Comprehensive Genomic Profiling of Solid Tumours

Northbeck Health Plan Medical Policy
Effective: 2026-01-01 | Version: 2.0 | Service line: Oncology
Review cycle: semi-annual | Next scheduled review: 2026-07-01

## NBH-ONCO-063-1 — Scope

This policy addresses coverage of comprehensive genomic profiling performed on
tissue or plasma for members with advanced solid tumours, where the panel
assays fifty or more genes. Single-gene testing and hereditary cancer risk
testing are addressed under NBH-ONCO-061 and NBH-ONCO-058 respectively.

## NBH-ONCO-063-2 — Coverage Position

Northbeck Health Plan considers comprehensive genomic profiling medically
necessary once per distinct tumour diagnosis when the criteria in section 3 are
documented. Repeat profiling is subject to section 5.

## NBH-ONCO-063-3 — Coverage Criteria

All of the following must be documented in the member's medical record:

### NBH-ONCO-063-3.1

The member has a histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of a solid
tumour, documented by a pathology report.

### NBH-ONCO-063-3.2

The disease is documented as recurrent, metastatic, or otherwise advanced and
not amenable to curative-intent local therapy.

### NBH-ONCO-063-3.3

The treating oncologist has documented that the result may identify a therapy
with an established indication for the member's tumour type, or eligibility for
an open clinical trial, and has stated which decision depends on the result.

### NBH-ONCO-063-3.4

Sufficient tissue is available for the assay, or plasma-based testing is
documented as appropriate because tissue is insufficient or its acquisition
carries undue risk.

### NBH-ONCO-063-3.5

The member has a performance status consistent with receiving systemic therapy
should an actionable alteration be identified.

## NBH-ONCO-063-4 — Documentation Requirements

### NBH-ONCO-063-4.1

The pathology report confirming the diagnosis, with its date and the specimen
source.

### NBH-ONCO-063-4.2

Imaging or clinical documentation establishing the stage of disease relied upon
under section 3.2.

### NBH-ONCO-063-4.3

An oncology note stating the treatment decision that depends on the result.

## NBH-ONCO-063-5 — Repeat Testing

Repeat comprehensive genomic profiling for the same tumour is covered where
disease progression on systemic therapy is documented and the treating
oncologist documents that resistance mechanism testing will inform the next
line of therapy.

## NBH-ONCO-063-6 — Exclusions

### NBH-ONCO-063-6.1

Comprehensive genomic profiling performed for a member receiving hospice care
or where no systemic therapy is planned.

### NBH-ONCO-063-6.2

Profiling performed solely for the purpose of a registry, a database, or a
research protocol.

### NBH-ONCO-063-6.3

Panels assaying fewer than fifty genes, which are addressed under NBH-ONCO-061.

## NBH-ONCO-063-7 — Appeal Rights

An adverse determination under this policy may be appealed in writing within
180 days of the date of the determination notice. A first-level appeal receives
a written response within 30 calendar days. Given the time-sensitive nature of
oncology treatment decisions, an expedited appeal decided within 72 hours may
be requested by the treating oncologist.
