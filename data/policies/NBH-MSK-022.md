# NBH-MSK-022 — Magnetic Resonance Imaging of the Lumbar Spine

Northbeck Health Plan Medical Policy
Effective: 2026-01-01 | Version: 5.0 | Service line: Musculoskeletal
Review cycle: annual | Next scheduled review: 2027-01-01

## NBH-MSK-022-1 — Scope

This policy addresses coverage of magnetic resonance imaging of the lumbar
spine, with or without contrast, performed for the evaluation of low back pain,
radicular symptoms, or suspected structural pathology. Imaging of the cervical
and thoracic spine is addressed under NBH-MSK-023.

## NBH-MSK-022-2 — Coverage Position

Northbeck Health Plan considers magnetic resonance imaging of the lumbar spine
medically necessary when the criteria in section 3 are documented, or when any
single criterion in section 4 is documented.

## NBH-MSK-022-3 — Coverage Criteria: Non-Urgent Presentation

All of the following must be documented in the member's medical record:

### NBH-MSK-022-3.1

The member has low back pain, with or without radicular symptoms, that has
persisted for at least six weeks from the documented date of onset.

### NBH-MSK-022-3.2

The member has completed a documented trial of conservative management of at
least six weeks' duration. Conservative management means at least two of the
following: a supervised course of physical therapy, a prescribed home exercise
programme with documented adherence, pharmacologic management, or an
image-guided injection.

### NBH-MSK-022-3.3

The member's symptoms have failed to improve, or have worsened, following the
conservative management documented under section 3.2, and the medical record
states the clinical basis for that assessment.

### NBH-MSK-022-3.4

A focused neurological examination has been performed and documented within the
twelve weeks preceding the request, including motor strength, sensory testing,
and reflex assessment of the lower extremities.

### NBH-MSK-022-3.5

The treating clinician has documented that the imaging result is expected to
change management, including consideration for surgical referral or for an
image-guided intervention.

## NBH-MSK-022-4 — Coverage Criteria: Urgent Presentation

Where any one of the following is documented, the requirements of section 3 do
not apply and imaging is considered medically necessary without a conservative
management trial:

### NBH-MSK-022-4.1

Signs or symptoms of cauda equina syndrome, including new bowel or bladder
dysfunction, saddle anaesthesia, or bilateral lower extremity weakness.

### NBH-MSK-022-4.2

Progressive motor deficit documented on serial neurological examination.

### NBH-MSK-022-4.3

Known or suspected malignancy with new or progressive back pain.

### NBH-MSK-022-4.4

Suspected spinal infection, including documented fever with back pain,
intravenous drug use, or recent spinal instrumentation.

### NBH-MSK-022-4.5

Known or suspected vertebral fracture in a member with osteoporosis, chronic
corticosteroid use, or a documented traumatic mechanism.

## NBH-MSK-022-5 — Documentation Requirements

### NBH-MSK-022-5.1

A clinical note documenting the date of symptom onset, the character and
distribution of symptoms, and any red-flag findings considered and excluded.

### NBH-MSK-022-5.2

Physical therapy notes, a home exercise programme record, or pharmacy records
evidencing the conservative management relied upon under section 3.2, with
dates.

### NBH-MSK-022-5.3

The neurological examination documented under section 3.4, with its date.

## NBH-MSK-022-6 — Exclusions

### NBH-MSK-022-6.1

Repeat lumbar magnetic resonance imaging within twelve months of a prior
diagnostic-quality study of the same region, absent a documented new
neurological deficit or a documented interval change in clinical status.

### NBH-MSK-022-6.2

Imaging performed solely to satisfy a request from a member, an employer, or a
third party where no clinical indication is documented.

### NBH-MSK-022-6.3

Imaging performed for the purpose of establishing disability status where no
change in clinical management is anticipated.

## NBH-MSK-022-7 — Appeal Rights

An adverse determination under this policy may be appealed in writing within
180 days of the date of the determination notice. A first-level appeal receives
a written response within 30 calendar days.
