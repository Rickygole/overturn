DETERMINATION

Northbeck Health Plan has completed its review of the first-level written
appeal submitted on behalf of the member on June 25, 2026. The original
determination is UPHELD. Coverage for the service listed above remains DENIED.

Denial reason code: NBH-NMN-11-A1

Reason for determination:

  The appeal reviewer, a board-certified cardiologist who did not participate
  in the original determination, re-examined whether cardiac magnetic
  resonance imaging is medically necessary under the plan's applicable medical
  policy for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging. The reviewer considered the
  additional information submitted with the appeal, including the treating
  cardiologist's letter of April 22, 2026, the twelve-lead electrocardiogram,
  and the transthoracic echocardiogram report. The reviewer concurs with the
  original determination that the submitted records do not establish that a
  contraindication to magnetic resonance imaging has been addressed: the
  record identifies a permanent pacemaker without stating whether the device
  is MRI-conditional, and the appeal submission did not supply this
  information. On that basis the reviewer upholds the denial of cardiac
  magnetic resonance imaging and did not reach the remaining criteria under
  NBH-CARD-014-3.

  This determination was made by a licensed physician reviewer following
  review of the appeal record on July 20, 2026.
