Appeal review · Northbeck Health Plan

Nothing is transmitted until two people sign.

Behind this screen, drafted appeals wait for a human. Every citation in them has already been checked against the payer's own published policy text; what is left is the judgement a person has to make.

Demonstration system — everything here is synthetic

  • Northbeck Health Plan does not exist. It is an insurer invented for this project.
  • Its medical policies were written for this project, modelled on the structure real payers publish openly.
  • Every patient chart is generated by Synthea. No real patient data was used, and none was consulted.

Nothing on this system is medical, legal, or insurance advice, and no appeal is transmitted to any real insurer.

Printed in the project README, and here. This interface renders what looks like a medical record, so it has a door — but a judge is meant to walk through it, and there is nothing behind it that anyone should not see.

What is behind it

  • Clerk Confirms the paper trail — that the cited sections exist, that the quotes match their source, and that the letter asserts nothing the chart does not support.
  • Clinician Signs the clinical argument, on a separate screen. A billing clerk is the right person to approve paperwork and the wrong person to approve medicine.
  • Audit log Every screening finding, drafting attempt, rejection reason and signature, appended under the name entered with the decision.
